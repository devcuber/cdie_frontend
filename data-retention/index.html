Data Retention & Privacy

Privacy by Design. Your Data, Your Control.

GerLab-AI processes laboratory reports and transforms them into structured clinical data. CDIE is not a permanent repository for clinical documents or patient records.

Temporary storage and automatic deletion

Your uploaded documents and extracted results are temporarily stored for up to 24 hours, then automatically deleted. Temporary storage lets you process, review and download your results.

Download and save your results to your device before they expire. After deletion, the documents and results are no longer available through GerLab-AI.

Download and save your results

Use Download CSV (Excel) beside a completed extraction request. Open the downloaded CSV in Excel and save it to your device. You can also download the JSON result.

Opening or reviewing results on the website does not save a local copy. Keep your downloaded files in a storage location you are authorized to use.

Early deletion — planned feature

A Delete Now feature is planned to let you delete uploaded documents and extracted results before automatic expiration. It is not available yet. Any disabled preview of this control does not send a deletion request.

Before uploading

Only upload documents you are authorized to share and process. Clinical documents may contain sensitive patient information.

Questions about your data

Contact luisangelro@cdie.io with questions about data retention, processing or deletion.
